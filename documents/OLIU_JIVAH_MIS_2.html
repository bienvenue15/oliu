Two books — no inter-company debt

OLIU and JIVAH keep separate books. Neither company is treated as owing the other money. Costs sit in the book of the company that paid them. There is no “JIVAH owes OLIU” balance, no settle button, and no elimination on a group pack.

Rwanda (OLIU) bills the client. JIVAH’s book holds Tanzania-side costs only — it does not raise the client invoice.

Why this is the fastest screen in the system

Tips happen at any stage, for any reason — sometimes twice in an hour. A two-minute form stops being used, and the reconciliation control depends on every tip being logged.

No approval gate on the way out. Nobody waits for a manager at a roadblock — the control happens afterwards, in reconciliation.

Several containers, one payment? Tap them all — the split arithmetic is the system's job, not the payer's.

Offline-first. Saves locally, syncs when signal appears — decisions Q4/Q5 settle whether this must hold up on a field device or only at a dispatcher's desk.

Analysis — what “the system does the invoice” means

  • Trigger. When a container reaches “departed” and its own costs are covered (handoff rule), the system opens or extends a draft AR invoice for that client. The dashboard figure “departed but not invoiced” is the count of boxes not yet on an issued invoice — the draft already exists; the Finance Officer opens it, checks the lines, and issues.
  • Bundling. One invoice can carry several containers across several bills of lading, exactly like KGLDAR03102026 (12 boxes, 2 BLs). Each container still resolves to its own revenue line for the container P&L.
  • Numbering. Today's numbers are date-based (KGLDAR03102026 = 03/10/2026): two invoices on the same day would collide and gaps cannot be seen. The system assigns a gapless sequence and may keep the KGLDAR prefix for continuity.
  • Rate. Both invoices in hand bill a flat USD 3,600 per 40FT box. The rate card holds this per client; a line that differs from the card is flagged on the draft, not silently accepted.
  • Issue gate. Invoice is issued only when every container on it has departed, costs are covered and the EBM/TRA fiscal receipt is attached. Issuing freezes the lines; later changes go through a credit note, never an edit.
  • Output. PDF in the current layout (header, bill-to, line table, sub-total, three bank blocks, Finance Officer name), emailed to the client from the system and stored on the container records. Nothing is retyped from the shipment sheet.
  • Validate with us: keep the KGLDAR prefix or move to INV-OL? Is the USD 3,600 flat rate the rule, or does it vary by route and size? Is “terms of payment — full payment” the only term, or do some clients get days?
